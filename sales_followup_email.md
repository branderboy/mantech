# Sales Follow-Up Email Templates

Reusable templates for the sales team. Copy, paste, fill the brackets, send.
The link should point to the **Buyer Brief** (home page) for most prospects, or **The Vision** page when selling the broader platform.

---

**Subject:** 20 minutes to see where your team's hours are going

You will see exactly how many hours your team loses to manual work today (permissions, data, briefings, search) and how much capacity that is worth back to the mission.

Here is the overview before we talk: [link]

Open to a 20-minute look next week?

[Rep name]

## Notes for reps
- **The ask is always the same:** the 20-minute Mission Capacity Assessment. Low risk, fast, uses the buyer's own numbers.
- **Lead with the buyer's job, not the tech.** Name the work their people actually do (permissions, data, briefings, search).
- **Keep the human in it.** "A person approves every step. We take the busywork, not the jobs."
- **One link, three uses:** present it live, send it as the follow-up, let them self-serve.
