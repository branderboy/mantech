Meet The Mission Assistant

Turn Manual, Repetitive Work Into Hours Your Team Gets Back.

A Mission Assistant is an AI worker that does one recurring job for you, granting folder permissions, building a briefing deck, finding a record. It works on your data inside your own environment, including closed and classified networks, and acts only after a person approves. Here is what that means in practice.

1

Runs Inside Your Network

Each Mission Assistant works on your own data, inside your own network, including closed and classified ones. Nothing leaves your environment, and you add no headcount.

2

Not a do-everything chatbot. Each Mission Assistant owns a single recurring job, granting folder permissions, building a briefing deck, finding a record, the way your best specialist would.

3

Your People Approve Every Move

The Mission Assistant does the work and shows the sources behind it. One of your people reviews and approves before anything happens. You decide what it can do on its own.
