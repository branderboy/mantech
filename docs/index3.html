The Architecture

Four Layers, One Workforce

Built in tiers, each with one job: assistants generate output, governance earns trust, data builds confidence. Scroll to build the stack.

Layer 1

Human Workforce

Mission leaders, program managers, engineers, analysts, planners, compliance professionals, and subject matter experts. They own judgment, approval, leadership, and the decisions that carry consequence.

Layer 2

Mission Assistant Layer

Dedicated assistants, each handling the recurring work of a single function under human supervision (e.g., MILCON Cost Estimators, Contract Review Assistants, RMF Compliance Assistants).

Layer 3

Governance Layer

Escalation controls, auditability, human approval, source traceability, performance monitoring, drift detection, security review, and operational oversight.

Layer 4

Mission Data Foundation

Governed enterprise data, authoritative sources, multicloud architecture, core to edge deployment, and containerized execution. The ground that makes an assistant worth trusting.

The Honest Part

AI Can Be A Real Risk. We Engineered The Risk Out.

You have heard the warnings, and most of them are fair. AI fails when it acts alone, unwatched, and unaccountable. So we built the Mission Assistant to remove each of those failure modes. The Mission Assistant takes the repetitive work. Your people keep judgment, approval, and the decisions that carry consequence, the part that was always the job, and it stays with them.

Meet the product doing it: each named Mission Assistant runs these same controls on a real job your teams need done.

Confidence Scores, Not Blind Trust

Every output is scored and shows its sources. High-confidence work moves; anything uncertain is flagged and routed to a person. Nothing questionable ships on its own, so no one is blindsided by a machine.

A Person Approves Every Move

No access to live systems until you authorize it. The Mission Assistant does one job, shows its work, and waits for a human to approve. By design, the system cannot run without your people.

Mission Alerts On Watch

Drift detection, performance monitoring, and security review run continuously. The moment something looks off, it escalates to a human, before it becomes an audit finding or a breach.

Support That Stays

We tune each assistant, train your people, and adjust thresholds as your environment and the threats change. There is always someone accountable behind the system, never a walk-away handoff.

All Of It On One Screen: The Mission Capacity Console

Scores, approvals, escalations, and mission alerts surface in a single dashboard a named person owns. A Program Lead approves the day's work, low-confidence items are held for review, and permission conflicts and policy changes are flagged the moment they appear. Nothing happens in the dark, and there is always a human accountable on the screen.

This Protects Jobs. It Does Not Replace Them.

The Mission Assistant carries the busywork. Your team keeps the judgment, the approvals, and the accountability, the work that actually requires them. A required human in the loop makes your workforce more valuable, not redundant. People do the mission; assistants do the chore.
